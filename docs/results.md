# Experimental Results

## Divergence-Aware Runtime Assurance for AI-Driven Digital Twins in Autonomous Logistics Systems

**Project:** DARA-DT — Divergence-Aware Runtime Assurance for Digital Twins  
**Experimental Programme:** EXP-001 to EXP-010  
**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Domain:** Autonomous Logistics Digital Twins  
**Primary Research Theme:** Trustworthy Intelligent Systems

---

# 1. Purpose

This document consolidates the experimental results of the DARA-DT research
programme.

The programme investigates the central research question:

> **How can physical–digital divergence be quantified at runtime and used to
> regulate autonomous AI decision-making in dynamic logistics Digital Twins?**

The experiments progressively investigate:

1. physical–digital divergence;
2. decision relevance;
3. divergence severity;
4. decision impact and validity;
5. runtime evidence reliability;
6. cross-dependency generalisation;
7. uncertainty-aware runtime assurance;
8. decision-conditioned evidence uncertainty; and
9. multi-stage divergence propagation.

The purpose of this document is to report what the experiments actually
demonstrate.

Positive, negative and equivalence results are retained.

No experimental result is reinterpreted solely to preserve a proposed
DARA-DT advantage.

---
